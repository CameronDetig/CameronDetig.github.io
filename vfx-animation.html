VASC Sea Turtle VR Project

During my undergraduate degree I worked as part of UNC Wilmington’s Virtual Access to STEM Careers team funded by the National Science Foundation. Created an educational virtual reality experience deployed in classrooms to teach elementary schoolers about sea turtles and their preservation. Utilized C# and the SteamVR API in Unity for development.
